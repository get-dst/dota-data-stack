---
metric: contest_rate
summary: Share of pro matches in which a hero was picked or banned.
about: hero_draft_stats.contest_rate
aliases: [contested, pick+ban rate, presence]
grain: one value per hero, over a set of matches
---

Picks plus bans of the hero, divided by matches in the same window. The usual measure
of how much the pro meta revolves around a hero: a hero with a low pick rate and a high
ban rate is contested, not unpopular.
