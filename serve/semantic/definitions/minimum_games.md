aliases: [sample size, enough games, small sample, at least N games]

Win rates, matchup rates and duo rates are ratios of small integers. A hero seen in one
game has a win rate of 0 or 1, and neither means anything. So: when ranking heroes, pairs
or matchups by a rate, keep only those with at least 20 games unless the question sets
its own floor, and state the game count beside every rate so the reader can judge it. A
question about one specific hero at one bracket is answered whatever the count, with the
count stated and a note when it is under 20.
